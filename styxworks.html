HOW THE RIVER WORKS ▸

Arrivals → Queue → Engines → Obols. Souls stream in, wait in berths, and your engines transcribe them into memories Charon pays for. Engines can only transcribe souls that are actually waiting.

Arrivals: 0.8/s base.

Starving (queue empty): engines idle — income is capped by arrivals, not engine power. Fix it by buying engines (each one also raises arrivals) or waiting for the ferry.

Overflow (queue full): new souls have nowhere to berth and are forgotten — gone for good, counted above. Fix it with more engines or more berths.

The ferry docks every few minutes with 45 seconds' worth of arrivals (min 30 souls) plus a tip worth 2× the payload. Unload it into a near-full queue and the excess still vanishes.

Your hands never starve: the TRANSCRIBE button pays even at an empty riverbank.

The Crossing

When the ledger groans, drink from the Lethe: forget every obol, machine, and upgrade — keep only shards and the Wards they bought. Each shard hums at +2% production, forever. Each crossing, the river offers you a choice of three Wards; take one.
